Q: The Earth pulls a book downwards with a force of 10 N. What force does the book exert on the Earth?
A: 10 N upwards
Accept: 10 N; 10 N towards the book; 10 N up

Q: Why is a door easier to open by pushing at the handle than by pushing near the hinge?
A: Larger distance from pivot gives larger moment
Accept: Bigger distance from hinge, bigger moment; larger moment for the same force; needs less force for same moment
Ref: 9.6P

Q: State the equation linking moment, force and distance.
A: Moment = force × perpendicular distance
Accept: Moment = force × distance normal to the force; M = F × d; moment = force × distance
Ref: 9.7P
D: 2

Q: What is the unit of a moment?
A: Newton metre
Accept: N m; Nm; newton metres
Ref: 9.7P
D: 1
Note: Not N/m, which is the unit of spring constant.

Q: A 20 N force acts at right angles 0.5 m from a pivot. What is its moment?
A: 10 N m
Accept: 10 Nm; 10 newton metres
Ref: 9.7P
D: 2

Q: A 0.25 m spanner must give a moment of 40 N m. What force is needed at right angles at its end?
A: 160 N
Accept: 160 newtons
Ref: 9.7P
D: 3
Note: Force = moment ÷ distance = 40 ÷ 0.25.

Q: When an object is balanced, the total anticlockwise moment equals the total of which other moments?

Q: State the principle of moments.
A: Clockwise moments equal anticlockwise moments in equilibrium
Accept: Sum of clockwise moments = sum of anticlockwise moments; total clockwise moment equals total anticlockwise moment
Ref: 9.8P
D: 2

Q: A balanced beam has 10 N acting 0.3 m left of the pivot. What force acting 0.6 m right of the pivot balances it?

Q: A 300 N child sits 2 m from a seesaw's pivot. How far from the pivot must a 400 N child sit to balance it?
