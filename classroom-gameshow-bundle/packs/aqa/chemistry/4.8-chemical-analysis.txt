# 4.8.1.1 Pure substances

Q: In chemistry, what name is given to a single element or compound that is not mixed with any other substance?
A: Pure substance
Accept: Pure
Ref: 4.8.1.1
D: 1
Id: aqa-chem-4.8-001

Q: Pure water boils at 100 °C. A sample of water boils at 102 °C. Is the sample pure or impure?
A: Impure
Accept: Mixture; Not pure
Ref: 4.8.1.1
D: 2
Id: aqa-chem-4.8-002

Q: How does the melting behaviour of a pure substance differ from that of a mixture?
A: Pure substance melts at one specific temperature
Accept: Mixture melts over a range of temperatures; Pure has a sharp melting point
Ref: 4.8.1.1
D: 3
Id: aqa-chem-4.8-003

Q: In everyday language, what does "pure" mean, as in "pure milk"?
A: Nothing added; in its natural state
Accept: Unadulterated; Nothing has been added
Ref: 4.8.1.1
D: 2
Note: Contrast with the chemistry meaning: a single element or compound.
