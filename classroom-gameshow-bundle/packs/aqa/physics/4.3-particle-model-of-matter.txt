# 4.3.1.1 Density of materials

Q: What is the SI unit of density?
A: kg/m³
Accept: Kilograms per metre cubed; Kilograms per cubic metre; kg/m3; kg m⁻³
Ref: 4.3.1.1
D: 1
Note: g/cm³ is also a unit of density but not the SI unit.
Id: aqa-phys-4.3-001

Q: State the equation that links density, mass and volume.
A: Density = mass ÷ volume
Accept: ρ = m/V; Density = mass/volume; d = m/V
Ref: 4.3.1.1
D: 2
Id: aqa-phys-4.3-002
