# 4.4.1.1 Photosynthetic reaction

Q: Which gas do plants take in from the air to use in photosynthesis?
A: Carbon dioxide
Accept: CO₂; CO2
Ref: 4.4.1.1
D: 1
Id: aqa-bio-4.4-001

Q: Write the word equation for photosynthesis.
A: Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
Accept: CO₂ + H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂; 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Ref: 4.4.1.1
D: 2
Note: Light can be written over the arrow; it is not a reactant.
Id: aqa-bio-4.4-002

Q: Is photosynthesis an endothermic or an exothermic reaction?
A: Endothermic
Ref: 4.4.1.1
D: 1
Id: aqa-bio-4.4-003

Q: Explain why photosynthesis is described as an endothermic reaction.
A: Energy is transferred from the environment
Accept: Takes in energy from the surroundings; Absorbs light energy from the environment
Ref: 4.4.1.1
D: 3
Note: The energy is transferred to the chloroplasts by light.
