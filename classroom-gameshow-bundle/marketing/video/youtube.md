## Title

Showtime: 4 classroom game shows for whole-class GCSE Science revision (AQA & Edexcel)

## Description

Turn GCSE Science revision into a game show. Every student answers every question on a mini whiteboard, and you mark the whole class in seconds.

Four games in one file:
• Over the Edge: right answers win counters to drop down the pegs. Push the pile over the edge, then play for the jackpot together.
• Outpace: the whole class is one runner. Answer your way home before the Hunter catches you.
• Category Clash: teams pick a category and a points value. One hidden ★ tile is worth double.
• Hex Hunt: two halves of the class race to build a path across the hexagon board.

Science mega pack: Biology, Chemistry and Physics for AQA and Edexcel, Separate and Combined Science, with every question tagged to the specification. At the end of every game the class results show the questions most teams got wrong, so you know exactly what to reteach.

No logins, no accounts, no internet needed: it runs offline from a single file on your classroom computer.
