Teacher guide · version 1.8.1

Four whole-class revision games, inspired by classic TV quiz formats. One file, no internet, no logins, no installs.

The games

Over the Edge

Each correct team drops a counter down a lane; whatever it pushes over the edge is theirs. In the final the whole class plays for the £5,000 jackpot.

Outpace

The class is one runner. Pick a deal, answer your way home before the Hunter catches you, then beat the 90-second Final Sprint.

Category Clash

Teams take turns to pick a category and points value; everyone answers. Harder questions are worth more, and one hidden ★ tile is worth double.

Hex Hunt

Two halves of the class race to link their edges across a board of lettered hexagons. Each letter is the first letter of the answer (for a number, of the number said in words: 1236 is "one thousand", so O).

Teams answer on mini whiteboards: no pupil devices needed. Topics a team gets wrong come round more often.

Getting started

  1. Save showtime-classroom-gameshows.html on your computer and keep using that copy and browser (your own sets are saved there).
  2. Double-click it. Choose your subject, exam board and, for sciences, Separate or Combined Science. Under Questions tick your topics, or leave Mixed. Untick Higher tier only for a Foundation class.
  3. Pick a game, choose the number of teams (2 to 6) and press Start. The game starts at once, so hand out whiteboards first.

Every question

  1. The question stays up while teams write an answer. There is no countdown.
  2. Press Space for "3, 2, 1, show me!".
  3. Mark each team: tap its panel (or press its number) to toggle ✓/✗. C marks all correct. Confirm with Space.
  4. The answer appears and the game moves on. U undoes a slip.
Team rule: a team is correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct. You are the judge, so accept good alternatives.

Each game takes about 10 minutes (Maths longer). P pauses, F is full screen. End game goes straight to the results. Every game ends with Reteach these: the questions most teams got wrong, with answers and topics, to plan your next starter. Nothing is recorded about individual students.

Game notes

  • Outpace: the class holds up 1, 2 or 3 fingers for Cautious, Standard or Bold and you press the majority. If more than half the teams are correct the runner steps forward; otherwise the Hunter gains. The Final Sprint clock starts only when you press Start.
  • Category Clash: the choosing team wins full points, other correct teams half, and wrong answers lose nothing.
  • Hex Hunt: after "show me", press the half with more right answers (1 or 2, or N for neither). It wins the hexagon and picks next. Split the class into two evenly matched halves.

In play

Adding your own questions

Open Question bank, name the set, paste your questions in this format and press Save as new set:

Subject: Chemistry
Topic: Bonding
Q: What type of bonding is in sodium chloride?
A: Ionic
Accept: Ionic bonding
Q: Give two ways to increase the rate of a reaction.
A: Any two: increase temperature, concentration or surface area, add a catalyst
Note: Not "heat it up" on its own
  • Subject and Topic lines apply to every question below them. Keep topic names consistent: they drive the weak-topic tracking.
  • Optional: Accept: (other answers, separated by ;), Note: (for you), Difficulty: 1–3 (recall, describe and apply, explain and extend: Category Clash uses it for its rows) and Tier: Higher.
  • Built-in packs cannot be changed, but Copy to edit makes your own copy.
  • Save backup file keeps your sets and team history (saved by team name) in one file; Load backup file adds them on another computer without deleting anything.

Settings

Large text suits the back of the room. Reduced motion turns off camera moves, shake, confetti and flashes. Graphics: Low suits older PCs. Music plays on the main screen only.

Privacy

No data leaves the computer. Showtime makes no network connections: no analytics, tracking, accounts or adverts. Question sets, team names, history and settings stay in this browser only, and the school stays in control under UK GDPR. If you use pupils' names, treat backup files like any file containing pupil data, or use team names or initials.
