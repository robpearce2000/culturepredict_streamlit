Exam board

Over the Edge

Every team that answers right wins a counter to drop down a lane. Push the pile over the edge, then play for the jackpot together.

Outpace

The whole class is one runner. Pick a deal, then answer your way home before the Hunter catches you.

Category Clash

Teams take turns to pick a category and a points value, and every team answers. Harder questions are worth more.

Hex Hunt

Two halves of the class race to build a path across the hexagon board. Each letter is the first letter of the answer.

Subject and Topic lines apply to every question under them until the next Topic line. Each Q: line needs an A: line straight after it. Optional: a line such as Difficulty: 3 (1 easy to 5 hard) applies to the questions below it until the next Difficulty or Topic line. Category Clash puts level 1 questions on the 100 row, level 3 on the 200 row and level 5 on the 300 row, borrowing the nearest level when a row runs short. The other games ignore it, and sets without it still work.

About

Showtime: Classroom Gameshows, first edition, version .

Four revision games for the classroom, inspired by classic TV quiz formats: Over the Edge, Outpace, Category Clash and Hex Hunt. Game design and question packs by Rob Pearce.

The GCSE Combined Science starter packs are AQA-style practice questions written for Showtime. They are not produced or endorsed by AQA.

Privacy

Showtime makes no network connections. Question sets, team names, wrong-answer history and settings are saved only in this browser on this computer (localStorage). Use the backup in the Question bank to move them, and clear a team's history there at any time.
