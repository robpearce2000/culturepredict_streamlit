How to play
Two teams race across the board. Team 1 links the left edge to the right edge; Team 2 links the top to the bottom. Pick a hexagon: its letter is the first letter of the answer. Either team can buzz in. Get it right and the hexagon is yours, and you pick the next one. Get it wrong and the other team can answer. The first team to join its two edges wins the round.
Whole class: split the class into two halves (each can be several table groups). Everyone answers every hexagon on whiteboards. Enter roughly what share of each half got it right; the higher share wins the hexagon, and on a tie the half that chose it wins. The captain who chooses changes each turn.
Play as

Two halves: each half is one team on the board. A group counts as correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct.
