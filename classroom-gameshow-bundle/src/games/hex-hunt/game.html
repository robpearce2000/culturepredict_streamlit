How to play
Split the class into two halves (each can be several table groups). Team 1 links the left edge to the right edge; Team 2 links the top to the bottom. The choosing half picks a hexagon: its letter is the first letter of the answer. Everyone answers on whiteboards. Compare the whiteboards and press the half that had more right answers: it wins the hexagon and picks the next one. If it's level, the half that chose it wins; if nobody got it, press Neither. The first half to join its two edges wins.

Team rule: a group counts as correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct.
