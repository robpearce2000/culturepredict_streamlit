How to play
Get a question right to win a counter. Pick a lane from 1 to 4, watch it bounce through the pegs onto the moving top shelf, and every counter pushed over the front edge wins £100. Counters that fall off the sides are lost. Look out for the star wildcard counters. In the final, both teams join forces: every correct answer wins two counters, and pushing the gold jackpot counter over the edge adds £5,000.
Whole class: every team answers every question on whiteboards, and every correct team wins a counter. Each team's captain picks a lane, and the counters drop together. A counter pushed over the edge goes to the team whose counter last landed in that quarter of the shelf. In the final the whole class plays together for the jackpot.
