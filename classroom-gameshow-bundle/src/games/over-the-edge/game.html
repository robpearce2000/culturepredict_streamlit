How to play
Every team answers every question on whiteboards, and every correct team wins a counter. Each team's captain picks a lane from 1 to 4 and the counters drop together, bouncing through the pegs onto the moving top shelf. Every counter pushed over the front edge wins £100 for the team whose counter last landed in that quarter of the shelf; counters that fall off the sides are lost. Look out for the star wildcard counters. In the final the whole class plays together: every correct team wins two counters, and pushing the gold jackpot counter over the edge adds £5,000.

Team rule: a team is correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct.
