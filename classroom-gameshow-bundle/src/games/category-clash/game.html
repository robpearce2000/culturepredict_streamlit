How to play
Teams take turns to pick a category and a points value. Harder questions are worth more. Answer right to win the points; get it wrong and another team can steal them. One hidden ★ tile is worth double. The team with the most points when the board is clear wins.
Whole class: every team answers every question on mini whiteboards. The choosing team wins full points if correct; every other correct team wins half. Wrong answers lose nothing. The captain who chooses changes each turn.
