How to play
Teams take turns to choose a category and a points value, and every team answers on mini whiteboards. The choosing team wins full points if correct; every other correct team wins half. Wrong answers lose nothing. Harder questions are worth more, and one hidden ★ tile is worth double. The captain changes every turn. The team with the most points when the board is clear wins.

Team rule: a team is correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct.
