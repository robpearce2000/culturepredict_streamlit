▼ HUNTER
⚑ HOME

Answer right to edge home. Answer wrong and the Hunter closes in.

How to play
The whole class is one runner against the Hunter, and every team answers every question on whiteboards.
Deal Round: vote for a deal with 1, 2 or 3 fingers. A bigger deal gives the Hunter a shorter gap. If at least half the teams are right the runner steps forward; otherwise the Hunter gains. Reach home to bank the points; if you are caught you bank half.
Final Sprint: 60 seconds. Every correct team adds a step. Reach the target to escape with the pot.

Team rule: a team is correct only if its whiteboards agree on a correct answer, or if you judge it correct.
